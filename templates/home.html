02 / THE METHOD

Confidence isn't a clever line.
It's knowing what to say next.

Inside the guide, you'll learn C.A.R.E. — four simple principles that turn a blank screen into a thoughtful, low-pressure opening.

C
CONTEXT

Notice something real.

Start with a detail that actually belongs to the conversation.

A
AUTHENTICITY

Sound like yourself.

Say something you would comfortably say in person.

R
RESPECT

Make space for choice.

Drop the pressure and leave room for any response, including none.

E
EASE

Keep it easy to answer.

Give a natural opening instead of a speech or interrogation.
